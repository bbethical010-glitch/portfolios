## 🌟 The Big Idea: What Makes it Different?

### 🏢 The Old Way (Centralized Cloud)
When you upload a photo to a standard cloud service, you send it over the internet to a massive computer "warehouse" (a data center) owned by a tech company. If their servers crash, or if your monthly credit card payment fails, you are locked out of your own memories until they fix it or you pay up.

### 📱 The Easy Storage Way (Decentralized Peer-to-Peer)
Easy Storage Cloud skips the middleman entirely. Instead of uploading your files to a corporate server on the internet, **your Android phone acts as your personal cloud warehouse.** 
When you want to download a movie from your phone to your laptop at work, your laptop speaks **directly** to your phone across the internet. Your files stay exactly where you put them—on your physical device.

## ✨ The Latest 2026 Upgrades (Your New Superpowers)

We have recently overhauled the core engines of the app to add powerful new capabilities:

### 🚀 1. Real-Time Multi-User Activity Feeds
Your personal cloud is no longer a silent folder. Now, when friends or coworkers access your files, you will see a live dashboard update instantly! You can see **how many users are connected right now** and track shared uploads as they happen. It makes your personal phone feel like a living, breathing collaborative workspace.

### 🧩 2. "Jigsaw Puzzle" File Delivery (Smart Chunking)
Previously, sending extremely large files (like long 4K vacation videos or massive zip archives) across the internet was risky; a tiny internet hiccup could ruin the whole transfer. 
Now, the app uses **5MB Smart Chunking**:
* Think of your huge file as a jigsaw puzzle.
* The app breaks the file into 5MB "pieces."
* Each piece is numbered and sent across securely.
* If your Wi-Fi blinks for a second, the transfer doesn't fail! It simply picks back up where it left off and reassembles the puzzle perfectly on your phone.

### 🔋 3. Unstoppable Background Transfers (Foreground Service)
On older apps, if you closed the screen or locked your phone, your file transfers would suddenly freeze. 
With our new **Native Foreground Service**, your transfers are protected! A steady notification icon will appear at the top of your phone, ensuring Android grants the app maximum power to finish your heavy downloads, even while your phone is sitting locked in your pocket.

### 🛑 4. Auto-Lock Safeguards (24-Hour Expiry)
Security is now stronger and smarter. If you log in to grab a file from a public library computer or a friend's laptop, the app will automatically expire that specific access token after **24 hours**. You never have to worry about accidentally leaving a device permanently connected to your personal cloud.

## 👥 How Security & Permissions Work (Explained Simply)

Giving someone a link to your phone sounds scary, so we built a strict security wall around your folders. Here is how it works:

1. **The 6-Digit Secret Key**: Every time you turn on your cloud, your phone creates a randomized 6-digit "Share Code." Nobody can even ask to connect unless you give them this code.
2. **The Heavy Scrambler (PBKDF2)**: When you set a password, the app runs it through a digital blender 120,000 times before storing it! This makes it practically impossible for hacker computers to "guess" your password.
3. **The "Access Cards" (Role-Based Access Control)**: You don't have to hand over the keys to the kingdom. When sharing a folder, you can choose their specific access card:
   * **👀 Viewers**: Can look at and download files. They cannot delete, move, or change anything. Excellent for sharing a wedding album with family.
   * **✍️ Contributors**: Can see files and add their own new ones, but cannot mess with your existing files. Perfect for letting your team turn in their homework assignments.
   * **🛠️ Managers**: Can create folders, rename files, and delete bad items, but cannot touch the master system settings.
   * **👑 Admin**: Full, unrestricted control (This is always YOU!).
